FOUND FOOTAGE · PRIVACY

Last updated: November 16, 2026.

The short version

Found Footage collects nothing. There is no account, no server of ours, no analytics and no advertising.

Your videos

Everything you film stays on your iPhone, inside the app. A video leaves your phone only when you choose to save it to Photos, post it or send it.

Permissions

Camera and microphone: to film your shots.
Photos (add only): to save a film when you ask. The app cannot read your library.
Notifications: optional, for reminders and the daily "You found a tape" message. They are scheduled on your phone.

The clock

To keep the daily battery honest, the app sometimes reads the time from an Apple server. Nothing about you is sent.

Purchases

Found Footage Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
