YOUR DAY, AS FOUND FOOTAGE.

Found Footage is an iPhone camera with a Y2K camcorder look. Every day it has 60 seconds of battery. Every day a tape is found: your last 24 hours, cut like footage someone dug out of a box.

Made for TikTok and Reels

One tap saves the tape, copies a caption and opens TikTok or Instagram. Vertical, a minute or less, a new one every day.

The rules

60 seconds of battery per tape. 10 seconds per shot, at most. One redo per shot. No import. No editing.

Private by design

No account. No server of ours. Your videos stay on your phone until you choose to post them.
