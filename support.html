FOUND FOOTAGE · SUPPORT

How it works

Every day your camera has 60 seconds of battery. Film shots of 10 seconds or less. At 6 PM the tape of your last 24 hours is found, and the battery recharges. Watch the tape, then post it, send it or keep it on the shelf.

I made a mistake in a shot

Right after a shot, a label offers one redo. The shot is erased, its seconds come back, and you film again. Pro gives 10 redos per shot.

The battery is dead

It recharges when the next tape is found: 6 PM, or the hour you chose in Pro (MENU, TAPE FOUND AT).

I can't open an old tape

In the free version the shelf plays the tapes of the last 7 days. Older tapes are never deleted: they stay on your phone and open again with Pro.

TikTok or Instagram does not open

The film is saved to Photos and the caption is copied. If the app does not open, pick it in the share list, or open it yourself and choose the latest video in your library.

Restore Pro

MENU, PRO, RESTORE PURCHASE. Use the Apple account that bought it.
